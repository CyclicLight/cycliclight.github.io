Born in [the wake of battling gods](/events#the-war-of-the-divines), the world is not always pleasant or safe. The [sea falls](/locations#the-dead-sea) & [devils walk](/locations#the-lands-of-ash).

In the north, [nature rules](/locations#the-living-city) and conquers. To the south people conquer. And everywhere they [suffer the consequences](/creatures#mutations).
